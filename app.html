What the numbers mean

Base weight is everything in the pack except food, water and fuel - the part you can actually fix with gear choices. Under 4.5 kg is ultralight, under 9 kg lightweight, under 13.6 kg traditional. Total pack weight is what your back feels at the trailhead, consumables included. The old rule of thumb says keep that under about 20% of your body weight; above 30% the walk stops being fun. The fastest savings come from the big three - pack, shelter and sleep system - so anything over its category benchmark gets flagged.
